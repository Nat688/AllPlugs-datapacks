Coals to Gunpowder

Smelt Coal or Charcoal into Gunpowder while staying true to vanilla Minecraft.

Type Datapack / Mod
Version 1.16.x - 26.2
Status Released

About

Coals to Gunpowder lets you smelt Coal or Charcoal into Gunpowder using a Furnace or Blast Furnace.

It provides a balanced alternative to Creeper farming and makes Gunpowder easier to obtain for TNT, Fireworks and other recipes.

Designed to stay faithful to vanilla Minecraft, it integrates naturally with existing smelting mechanics.

Installation

1. Download the datapack.
2. Place it inside the datapacks folder.
3. Run /reload.
4. Smelt Coal or Charcoal to obtain Gunpowder.

FAQ

Does it work on servers?

Yes. It works in both singleplayer and multiplayer.

Can I use Charcoal?

Yes. Both Coal and Charcoal are supported.

Does it require a resource pack?

No. No resource pack is required.
